New filter

Only search and auto-apply to jobs LinkedIn can submit in-app (no external site). The bot already clicks through "Next" / "Review" / "Continue" automatically until it reaches "Submit application".

Apply to every job found with Easy Apply, regardless of fit — no scoring, no "not relevant" skips. Higher volume, no relevance guarantee. (Easy Apply itself is still required — there's no way to auto-submit a job that only offers an external-site application.)

For every job found, draft an email to a best-guess company address (e.g. hr@company.com — not verified) with your resume attached. Drafts land in Outreach — nothing sends until you select and approve them there.

For every job found, look up your 1st-degree LinkedIn connections at that company and draft a referral-ask message to up to 3 of them. Also lands in Outreach for approval — nothing is sent to your real connections until you approve it. Sending needs automation running with LinkedIn logged in.
